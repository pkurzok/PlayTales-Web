## Boilerplate

**One sentence**

> PlayTales is an audio play player for children that makes the Apple Music catalogue simple enough
> for a child to work on their own.

**Short — about 50 words**

> PlayTales hands children the Apple Music catalogue of audio plays in an interface they can work
> themselves: big covers to tap instead of lists, titles read aloud for anyone who can't read yet,
> and a sleep timer that ends the episode on its own. Anything downloaded plays without an internet
> connection.

**Long — about 100 words**

> PlayTales is an audio play player for children, drawing on the full Apple Music catalogue. In
> place of lists and menus it shows big, colourful covers a child can tap straight away, and it
> reads artists and episodes aloud for anyone who can't read yet. A sleep timer ends the story by
> itself, and episodes downloaded through Apple Music keep playing with no signal at all — in the
> car, on the train, on holiday. Favourites and playback positions sync through the family's own
> private iCloud. Parents decide how much their child may listen each day or week — in minutes or
> in episodes — and keep the settings behind a PIN. PlayTales was written by Peter Kurzok, a father of three, originally for his own family.

## New in version 1.10.0

* **Episodes, not minutes** — parental controls can now limit listening to a number of episodes
  per day or week, alongside or instead of the time limit. An episode counts once it starts;
  carrying on with it costs nothing. "Parents: one more episode" grants an extra one. Which
  episodes were started stays on the device.
* **Played episodes** — episodes a child finishes get a checkmark on their cover. The sort menu can
  hide them, and pressing and holding a cover marks one by hand. The mark travels to the family's
  other devices through their private iCloud. Parents asked for it on the feature-request board.
* **"Next album" follows the sort order** — sorted by episode, episode 5 is now really followed by
  episode 6.

## New in version 1.9.0

* **Sort by episode** — a series' albums can be ordered by episode, release date, title or last
  played, and each series remembers its own order. Favourites sort by name, date added or last
  played. CarPlay follows the choice.

## New in version 1.8.0

* **Feature requests** — in Settings, parents suggest new features, vote on other people's ideas
  and comment on them (iOS 18 and later). The board sits in the parents' area, behind the math
  task or PIN. It asks for no name, e-mail address or account.
* **Easier to use** — larger tap targets, more precise VoiceOver labels, and a player that stays
  fully usable at the largest text size.
* **Smoother** — the offline view is prepared in the background without slowing the interface
  down.

## New in version 1.7.0

* **Discover new stories** — tap the search field and PlayTales suggests popular audio-play
  series, and ones similar to your favourites. An empty library gets something to start with
  straight away, too. The suggestions come from the Apple Music catalogue; which ones are tapped
  is not tracked.
* **Landscape and Split View** — PlayTales adapts: iPhone in landscape, Split View and Stage
  Manager on iPad, and the iPhone Duo.
* **Easier for everyone** — VoiceOver reads every screen, and Reduce Motion is respected.
* **iOS 17 again** — after the detour to iOS 18 in version 1.6.0, PlayTales runs on every device
  with iOS 17 again. Only Spotlight search still needs iOS 18.

## New in version 1.6.0

* **Limit listening time** — parents set a daily and a weekly allowance: 15 minutes to two hours a
  day, two to ten hours a week. Only actual listening counts, and it is counted on the device only —
  neither the allowance nor the time listened is transmitted. Parental controls are part of the free
  version.
* **"Ten more minutes" or "no limit today"** — when time is up, the child gets a friendly notice;
  only a parent can extend it. For long car journeys "Lift limits for today" releases both limits
  until midnight, and while CarPlay is connected they don't apply at all — no story is cut off
  mid-drive.
* **A PIN instead of a math task** — the settings can be guarded by a PIN of four to twelve digits;
  anyone who forgets it gets back in through a harder math task. Only a salted digest is stored,
  never the digits themselves.
* **A pointer to another app** — at the very bottom of the library the free version shows a single
  card pointing to an app by other independent developers. It sits below everything else,
  interrupts nothing, and is only visible if you scroll that far. No user or device identifier and
  no advertising ID reaches the service that places it, and no profile is built. "PlayTales Pro"
  removes the card entirely. What is transmitted is set out in the
  [privacy policy]({{ '/privacypolicy/' | relative_url }}).
* **Briefly iOS 18 only** — 1.6.0 needed iOS 18; later versions run on iOS 17 again.

## New in version 1.5.0

* **Listen offline** — without an internet connection PlayTales shows and plays only the stories
  Apple Music has already downloaded to the device. What isn't there isn't offered: for a child, an
  album that is tapped and then stays silent is the worse experience.
* **Sleep timer** — from five minutes to an hour, or until the end of the current episode.
* **At the end of the story** — stop, repeat the episode, or carry on with the artist's next album.
* **Play it with Siri** — "Play … in PlayTales", and as a Shortcut too.
* **Right in Spotlight** — favourites and recently played turn up directly in search (iOS 18 and
  later).
* **Progress across the whole story** — the slider can span the entire album rather than a single
  track.
* **In sync on every device** — favourites and playback positions travel through your private
  iCloud.

## Features

* Access to the full Apple Music catalogue of audio plays, audiobooks and music
* Big covers instead of lists — tile view or Cover Flow
* Artist and episode titles read aloud, for children who can't read yet
* Favourites and recently played, with playback positions down to the second
* Sort by episode, release date, title or last played
* Played episodes marked with a checkmark, and hidden on request
* Suggestions for new audio-play series, matched to your favourites
* Sleep timer and offline playback
* CarPlay support
* Parental controls: daily and weekly limits in minutes or episodes, a PIN or math task, and a
  switch to turn search off
* Landscape, Split View, Stage Manager and iPhone Duo
* AirPlay and Bluetooth, dark and light mode, VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion
* Feature requests: parents suggest new features and vote on them (iOS 18 and later)

**Free versus "PlayTales Pro":** the free version allows three favourites and shows one card at the
foot of the library pointing to another app. The one-time in-app purchase lifts that limit, unlocks
CarPlay, narrows the library offline to what can actually play, and removes the card. Parental
controls come with both. There is no subscription.

## About the developer

👋 Hi, I'm Peter Kurzok!

In my main job I work as a software engineer for an IT service provider and I specialize in iOS
apps. In my spare time, I enjoy music and photography and sometimes also create iOS apps 🙈

But most of my time is taken up by my family, more specifically my 3 children. That's why this app
exists. Strictly speaking, we only wrote PlayTales for ourselves. When the requests from other
friends with children started to pile up, we decided to publish PlayTales to the App Store.

So, say thank you and have fun using it 😅
