## Boilerplate

**One sentence**

> PlayTales is an audio play player for children that makes the Apple Music catalogue simple enough
> for a child to work on their own.

**Short — about 50 words**

> PlayTales hands children the Apple Music catalogue of audio plays in an interface they can work
> themselves: big covers to tap instead of lists, titles read aloud for anyone who can't read yet,
> and a sleep timer that ends the episode on its own. Anything downloaded plays without an internet
> connection.

**Long — about 100 words**

> PlayTales is an audio play player for children, drawing on the full Apple Music catalogue. In
> place of lists and menus it shows big, colourful covers a child can tap straight away, and it
> reads artists and episodes aloud for anyone who can't read yet. A sleep timer ends the story by
> itself, and episodes downloaded through Apple Music keep playing with no signal at all — in the
> car, on the train, on holiday. Favourites and playback positions sync through the family's own
> private iCloud. Parents keep the settings behind an arithmetic task small children can't solve.
> PlayTales was written by Peter Kurzok, a father of three, originally for his own family.

## New in version 1.5.0

* **Listen offline** — without an internet connection PlayTales shows and plays only the stories
  Apple Music has already downloaded to the device. What isn't there isn't offered: for a child, an
  album that is tapped and then stays silent is the worse experience.
* **Sleep timer** — from five minutes to an hour, or until the end of the current episode.
* **At the end of the story** — stop, repeat the episode, or carry on with the artist's next album.
* **Play it with Siri** — "Play … in PlayTales", and as a Shortcut too.
* **Right in Spotlight** — favourites and recently played turn up directly in search (iOS 18 and
  later).
* **Progress across the whole story** — the slider can span the entire album rather than a single
  track.
* **In sync on every device** — favourites and playback positions travel through your private
  iCloud.

## Features

* Access to the full Apple Music catalogue of audio plays, audiobooks and music
* Big covers instead of lists — tile view or Cover Flow
* Artist and episode titles read aloud, for children who can't read yet
* Favourites and recently played, with playback positions down to the second
* Sleep timer and offline playback
* CarPlay support
* A parents' area behind an arithmetic task, including a switch to turn search off
* AirPlay and Bluetooth, dark and light mode, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type

**Free versus "PlayTales Pro":** the free version allows three favourites. The one-time in-app
purchase lifts that limit, unlocks CarPlay, and narrows the library offline to what can actually
play. There is no subscription and no advertising.

## About the developer

👋 Hi, I'm Peter Kurzok!

In my main job I work as a software engineer for an IT service provider and I specialize in iOS
apps. In my spare time, I enjoy music and photography and sometimes also create iOS apps 🙈

But most of my time is taken up by my family, more specifically my 3 children. That's why this app
exists. Strictly speaking, we only wrote PlayTales for ourselves. When the requests from other
friends with children started to pile up, we decided to publish PlayTales to the App Store.

So, say thank you and have fun using it 😅
